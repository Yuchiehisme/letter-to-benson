A Letter to Benson

July 2026

Hey Benson,

I actually finished this letter after our video call on July 30. I had already written it on July 29, but after talking with you, I realized there were still a few things I wanted to say, so I added them here.

It's amazing—and honestly such a blessing—that I got to meet you simply because of a last-minute lunch with my friend. I never expected that coincidence would introduce me not only to you, but also to your religion.

Whether it was through our messages or visiting your church, I've learned so many new things—from religion, to English, to American culture. Of course, I also had the chance to meet the "elders and sisters" you always talked about. Talking with all of you was genuinely so much fun. You made what would have been a pretty ordinary summer vacation into something truly memorable.

I guess this is what you meant by "God's plan" (not Drake's God's Plan, haha). Seeing the gratitude and reverence that all of you have has also inspired me to become a kinder person and someone who appreciates the people and blessings in life even more. Thank you, truly.

I'm really glad that during the last part of your mission, we got to talk about so many things besides religion—music, sports, and so much more. You made me genuinely happy. It's been such a long time since I've talked with someone for hours like that. Honestly, you might even be the first person I've ever had that much to talk about, haha.

Since our paths crossed in such an unexpected way, I decided to give you my favorite book of all time, The Alchemist. (Taylor Swift's The Alchemy is also good though.)

It's a book that changed the way I see life, purpose, and the challenges we face. Whenever life gets difficult, it reminds me to keep believing and moving forward. It made me think that when you face setbacks, you'll always have Heavenly Father with you, while for me, this book has always been something that gives me faith and strength.

It was also such a funny coincidence that during our video call the day after I wrote this letter, you brought up the word "faith." That made me feel even more certain that I chose the right gift.

Finally, I wish both of us—the two freshmen—the very best as we begin this new chapter in college. I hope everything goes smoothly for you. Keep being the wonderful person you are, keep doing what you love, and may we both continue to grow and succeed in our own fields.

May the Force be with us!

Btw, you're seriously amazing at basketball. Meeting someone as cool as you has been such a unique experience—you honestly feel like the kind of guy who would show up in those Netflix series I watched, haha.

And one more thing... my favorite childhood athlete was actually Stephen Curry. I even bought his basketball shoes and wore them in many games. Ngl, in one of those games, I made the last two free throws to complete a comeback victory.

Anyway, thank you for being part of one of the most unforgettable summers of my life. I'll always be grateful that our paths crossed. Until we meet again.

With gratitude,
Abbie Huang
